Digital Delivery Policy

“Trust Your Subconscious: 21 Days” is a digital product. There is no shipping, courier, physical packaging, or shipping fee.

How delivery works

After a successful Razorpay payment, we send an email to the address entered at checkout with a link to the programme folder. The folder contains the “Hear Me First” introduction, 21 guided audio sessions, the ebook, and the workbook. You can open the link online and download files to your own device.

When to expect it

Delivery is normally automated shortly after payment is confirmed. If you do not see the email within 30 minutes, check your spam or promotions folder, then write to swetha@paepoi.com. Include your checkout email and payment reference so we can find the order and send the access link again.

Download help

Open the link in a browser, choose the file you want, and use Google Drive’s Download option. Large audio files may take longer depending on your internet connection. If you cannot open a file or the link asks for permission, contact us and tell us what you see.

Access and support

Please do not share the folder link outside your household or distribute the files. For access problems, contact swetha@paepoi.com. Refund requests are handled under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
